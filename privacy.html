Privacy Policy - Clip Poster

Clip Poster is a personal tool run by its owner to upload short video clips to the owner's own TikTok account.

What we collect: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token, your TikTok open ID and basic profile info (user.info.basic). These are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to upload videos to that same account.

What we don't do: we don't sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, we don't track visitors, and we don't collect data from anyone other than the account owner.

Deleting data: disconnect the app in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) and the stored token stops working; the owner deletes the local token file on request.
